Our Mission

Protecting the wild, one habitat at a time

Founded on a simple belief — that every creature deserves a place on this planet — WildGuard partners with local communities, scientists, and rangers to safeguard the world's most vulnerable ecosystems.

Your support funds anti-poaching patrols, habitat restoration, and rescue programs that bring animals back from the brink.

Meet the Wild

Species we're fighting for

A handful of the incredible animals our field teams protect every single day.

African Elephant

Poaching and habitat loss have cut their numbers by nearly 90% in a century. Our rangers patrol 400,000 acres of protected land.

Critically at risk

Snow Leopard

With fewer than 7,000 left in the wild, snow leopards depend on our camera-trap research and conflict-free communities.

Vulnerable

Hawksbill Turtle

Nearly every turtle rescued and rehabilitated by our coastal teams makes it back to the sea — but nesting beaches are disappearing.

Critically endangered

Bengal Tiger

Only around 4,500 remain. Every tiger we track through our collar program gives us the data to protect their corridors.
